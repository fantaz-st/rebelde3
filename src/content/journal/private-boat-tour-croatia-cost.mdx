# How Much Does a Private Boat Tour in Croatia Cost?

If you're planning a holiday on the Croatian coast, one of the first questions you probably have is:

**How much does a private boat tour in Croatia actually cost?**

The short answer:

**Anywhere from €500 to over €2,500 per day.**

The longer answer depends on the kind of experience you're looking for.

A private boat tour from Split is not simply about renting a boat for a few hours. The boat, the skipper, the itinerary, the destinations, and the freedom you have during the day all influence the final price.

At [Rebelde Boats](/), we believe the value of a private Adriatic experience is not measured only by how many places you visit, but by the quality of the day you spend on the water.

---

## What Determines the Price of a Private Boat Tour in Croatia?

Private boat tours are not all the same.

Two companies can offer a "full-day boat tour in Croatia" and provide completely different experiences.

The biggest factors affecting the price are:

- Boat size and comfort
- Number of guests
- Tour duration
- Fuel consumption
- Distance travelled
- Destinations visited
- Experience of the skipper and crew
- Season
- Additional services such as drinks, restaurant reservations, or water activities

A comfortable 12–13 metre motorboat designed for private groups will naturally cost more than a smaller speedboat.

But the difference is not only the boat.

It is the comfort, stability, privacy, and freedom that come with having the Adriatic to yourself.

These tours usually include:

- Coastal cruising
- Relaxing away from crowded beaches

From Split, popular half-day destinations include:

- Blue Lagoon
- Šolta

It is a perfect choice for guests who want a few hours of Mediterranean escape.

## Does the Price Include Fuel?

This is one of the most important questions to ask when comparing private boat tours in Croatia.

Some operators advertise a lower charter price and add fuel separately.

Others include fuel depending on the route and destination.

Before booking, always check what is included:

- Skipper
- Fuel
- VAT
- Insurance
- Drinks
- Snorkeling equipment
- Towels
- Restaurant arrangements

The cheapest advertised price is not always the best value if important costs are added later.

## Choosing the Right Private Boat Charter

When comparing boat tours in Croatia, price should only be one part of the decision.

### Choose the right boat for your group

A family with children, a couple looking for a romantic experience, and a group of friends celebrating together will all have different needs.

The right boat should feel comfortable for the way you want to spend your day.

### Choose an experienced local skipper

A good skipper does much more than drive the boat.

They understand:

- Weather conditions
- Sea currents
- The best times to visit popular locations
- Quiet places away from crowds
- Local restaurants and hidden corners

Those small details often make the biggest difference.

### Choose flexibility over a fixed schedule

The Adriatic changes every day.

Wind, waves, crowds, and your own preferences should influence the route.

A private boat tour should feel like freedom, not another schedule to follow.
