The Blue Cave (Modra špilja) sits on the small island of Biševo, roughly 40 nautical miles from Split. For a few hours around midday, sunlight enters the cave through an underwater opening, refracts off the sandy bottom, and fills the chamber with an unforgettable electric blue glow.

## Why the timing matters

The blue effect depends on sun position, wave state, and the number of boats already inside. This section will cover exactly when to plan your visit, what alternative caves offer similar magic, and why some tours skip the Blue Cave entirely on rough-sea days.
